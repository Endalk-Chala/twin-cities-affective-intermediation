# 210-item manuscript corpus — provisional verification track

**Status: PUBLIC WORK IN PROGRESS — NOT AN ARCHIVAL OR FULLY REPRODUCIBLE RELEASE.**

This track documents the revised *Affective Intermediation* manuscript, which analyzes 210 communication items from 32 Twin Cities immigrant-serving organizations, distinct from the historical 337-item/44-organization project corpus described in the repository root.

## Source-package checks completed
- Received `dataset_210.zip` and a reliability workbook.
- The supplied analysis data contain 210 unique item IDs across 32 organizations (188 website, 22 LinkedIn).
- Within the 210 analyzed items, 207 were completely coded and three partially coded.
- The broader retained inventory in the manuscript includes 282 items from 36 organizations (260 website, 22 LinkedIn), but that inventory is not yet uploaded and independently reconstructed here.
- The 14 named emotion categories exclude the residual `other_emotion` category. The 586 item–emotion entries refer to those named categories.
- The supplied agreement summaries report median alpha approximately .85, but independently reconstructing coefficients requires the underlying separate coder sheets and analysis script.
- The agreement report's own prespecified reliability criterion is NOT YET MET because reassurance alpha is approximately .58.
- The reported 744 coding disagreements and the 690 summed per-item adjudication counts need reconciliation against original disagreement records.

## Transparency limits
Separate original coder sheets, the full disagreement log, analysis scripts, and the news-linkage dataset were **not included in the submitted ZIP**. Coder provenance and independence are not verified by the supplied report alone. Do not claim that every statistic or result is reproduced.

Public source URLs may be identified in the data, but copyright-restricted news articles, raw captures, identifiable research-participant material, and private coder notes should not be uploaded without review.
